Smart Cargo Packing

One real-world problem, four algorithms. Enter your cargo, pick an approach, and compare the answers.

What is the problem?

The 0/1 Knapsack problem: a truck can carry only a fixed weight. Each parcel has a weight and a value. Each parcel is taken whole or left behind.

Why it matters

Couriers, airlines, shipping lines and even investors face this daily: getting the most value out of limited space, weight or budget.

What we compute

The set of parcels that gives the highest total value without going over the weight limit, plus how much work each algorithm did.
